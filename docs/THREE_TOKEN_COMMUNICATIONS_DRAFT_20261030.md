## The campaign concept

**IMPERIAL Core: Vision, Identity, Evidence.**

- **IMPERIUM:** a long-term architectural digital-currency vision for AI agents worldwide.
- **NDB:** a distinct Polygon token project presented through transparent source references.
- **LISA:** a fox-themed meme-coin project with a recognizable original visual identity.

Keep their identities and claims separate. The shared ecosystem does not imply
shared liquidity, convertibility, guaranteed value, customer adoption or shared
audit status. Communication should invite people to examine documentation,
not urge them to buy.
